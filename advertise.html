How it works

From enquiry to broadcast.

A simple process, with the station confirming the details at each step.

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Call, send a WhatsApp message or use the contact form to explain what you would like to promote.

  2. 2

    Receive a quotation

    The station confirms availability and provides its own pricing and options. No prices are set on this website.

  3. 3

    Prepare the message

    Agree the wording, duration and any production support with the station before it is scheduled.

  4. 4

    Broadcast

    The station schedules the approved advertisement or announcement according to the agreed arrangement.

What to include in your enquiry.

A few details help the station respond with useful options the first time.

  • Your business or organisation name
  • A phone number or email where the station can reach you
  • What you want to promote, in one or two sentences
  • Any preferred dates or period for the campaign
  • Whether you need production help or have a finished recording

All advertising and announcements are subject to the station's approval and applicable law. Political advertising is accepted only where it complies with the law and station policy. This proposal does not promise audience figures or campaign results.
