Community announcements

Share a notice with the community.

Condolences, lost and found items, meetings, church, school and public notices can be submitted for review. Station staff decide what is broadcast or published; submitting a notice does not guarantee it will be used.

What can be announced

Notices the station can review.

  • Condolences
  • Lost items
  • Found items
  • Community meetings
  • Church announcements
  • School announcements
  • Public events
  • Organisation announcements
  • Public notices

Submit an announcement.

Give the station enough detail to understand the notice. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

Include names, dates, places and any wording the station should use. Do not include private information that should not be broadcast.

This proposal site does not store submissions. Pressing send opens WhatsApp with your details so you can review and send them to the station yourself. Nothing is published automatically.

Review process

Every notice is checked first.

  1. You send the request to the station team.
  2. Station staff review the wording, details and suitability.
  3. The station decides whether, when and how to broadcast or publish it.

Submission does not guarantee broadcast or publication, and does not create a booking or payment.

Your information

Handled with care.

The details you type here are used to prepare a message to the station. This website does not save them. WhatsApp or your email app handles the message you choose to send, under its own privacy terms.

Only include personal details that are necessary for the announcement.
