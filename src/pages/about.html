The station

Radio with the community at its centre.

Radio Togotane 88.3 FM is a community radio station in Kehancha, Migori County, Kenya. This page tells you what we broadcast, who we broadcast for, and what we are still confirming.

At a glance.

The station broadcasts on 88.3 FM from Kehancha. Its published line-up runs 05:00–00:00 on weekdays, 05:00–21:00 on Saturday and 05:00–22:00 on Sunday.

Who the station serves.

The primary audience is the community in and around Kehancha, and listeners across Migori County. The station also serves Kenyans living in other regions and online listeners further afield, alongside local businesses and organisations looking to reach the community.

That mix shapes the website: local information comes first, programme details are quick to scan on a phone, and advertising enquiries are easy to start without a long form.

  • Listeners in Kehancha and surrounding communities
  • Listeners across Migori County
  • Kenyans living in other regions

Design principles

What this website is built around.

These are the choices behind this website, not claims about the station's history.

01

Local first

Kehancha, Migori County and the station's own voices lead the page, ahead of generic radio content.

02

Clear information

Programmes, times and presenters are presented plainly, with the published schedule separated from live status.

03

Light on data

No frameworks, no trackers and locally hosted fonts and images, so pages stay usable on modest phones and connections.

04

Nothing invented

Presenter photographs, news stories and events are marked as pending until the station supplies them.

About this site

Written by the station, kept honest.
