    The station

    A local voice for Kehancha and Migori County.

    Radio Togotane 88.3 FM serves listeners in Kehancha and the surrounding communities, with programming for Migori County and for Kenyans listening from further afield. Its published line-up runs from 05:00 to midnight on weekdays, and from 05:00 on Saturday and Sunday.

    Presenter names for some weekend programmes are still being confirmed with the station.
