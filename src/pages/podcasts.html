    How listen-back works.

    The station's recorder saves the online stream in hourly files, using East Africa Time, and updates this page from the files it has written.

    Programme names and presenter names come from the published schedule. In a recording they are labels, not proof that a named presenter was on air.

    If the stream goes offline, that time is missing from the archive. Nothing is filled in, re-created or simulated.

    The archive belongs to the station. Music and third-party material in a recording may need clearance before the file is published more widely.

    Automatic capture

    Start it once, and the archive fills itself.

    The recorder runs beside the live stream on a computer that stays online. It writes a new file every hour without anyone adding episodes by hand.

    If the recorder stops, no new files appear. Recordings already saved stay available.
